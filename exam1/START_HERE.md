# Start here

1. Try the [foundation check](diagnostic/questions.md) without notes. Use the [print PDF](diagnostic/questions.pdf) if you prefer paper; write "not sure" when needed.
2. Check the [answers](diagnostic/answers.md). Pick one area you missed, guessed, or could not explain.
3. Follow the matching [study path](study_paths.md). Try a fresh question after reviewing.

For help, use one [hint](hints.md), close it, and retry. If you still feel stuck, show your TA the step you tried.

The [main README](../README.md) has setup, test commands, and direct links to every resource. Heaps are optional until your instructor confirms coverage.
